Story

Four chapters, from Hans Pedersen of Skee to Alex, plus a short version and a change log. Uncertain points stay labeled. Living members of the current household are left out on purpose.

  1. 1A Boy Called Hans Pedersen of Skee (1833–1847)Hans Pedersen is born outside marriage at Valsømagle, grows up as a foster son in a wheelwright's household in Skee, and is confirmed in 1847.
  2. 2Bjernede: A Marriage, Four Children, and Loss (1862–1884)Hans Pedersen marries Ane Rasmusdatter, two of their sons die as infants, Hans Peter is born, and the father dies in 1867. Ane remarries, both she and her second husband die in 1883, and the matr. 5a estate is sold.
  3. 3Hans Peter Crosses to Minnesota (1884–1922)Hans Peter emigrates in 1884, marries Maren Sophia Jensen, farms and later dairy-farms his own land in Brookville Township, and raises eight children.
  4. 4From Elmer to Alex (1906–present)Elmer and Caroline, Donald and Janice, Michael, and Alex.
