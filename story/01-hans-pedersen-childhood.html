Chapter 1 — A Boy Called Hans Pedersen of Skee (1833–1847)

This chapter follows Hans Pedersen (P18), the father of the emigrant Hans Peter Petersen. Everything here comes from Danish church books and censuses. No family memory reaches back this far.

Born at Valsømagle

Hans Pedersen was born on 25 February 1833 at Valsømagle, a village in Haraldsted parish, Ringsted district, Sorø county, on the island of Zealand (S51, S52, S53). He was baptized in Haraldsted church on 31 March 1833, which was Palm Sunday (S51, S52).

The parish clerk entered him as uægte, meaning born outside marriage (S51, S52). His mother was Ane Marie, an unmarried servant from Valsømagle (S51, S56). Her father's name, and therefore her full surname, is written differently in different records, so this story does not settle it yet. The church book names a man as the child's father, but the records do not prove who he was, so he is not named here.

A foster son in Skee

Hans did not grow up with his mother. On the census of 18 February 1834, when he was one year old, he was living in the village of Skee in Haraldsted parish. He was listed as a pleiebarn (foster child) in the household of Jens Nielsen, a cottager (husmand) and wheelwright, and his wife Kirsten Johansdatter (S54).

He was still there at the next two censuses:

  • 1 February 1840: "Hans Pedersen, 6, unmarried, foster child" in the household of Jens Nielsen, 49, and Kirsten, 44 (S55).
  • 1 February 1845: "Hans Pedersen, 12," born in the parish, now called a pleiesøn (foster son) (S57).

So from his first year until at least age twelve, the cottage of Jens and Kirsten in Skee was his home (S54, S55, S57). No record found so far shows that the foster parents were related to him or to his mother.

The records show that Jens Nielsen worked as a wheelwright (S54). Many years later, Hans himself would be a wheelwright, a hjulmand (S36, S37). The sources do not say who taught him the trade. We can only note that he grew up in a wheelwright's home.

Confirmation, 1847

On 11 April 1847, the first Sunday after Easter, Hans was confirmed in Haraldsted church. He was entered as boy no. 3: "Hans Pedersen of Skee," born 25 February 1833 at Valsømagle and vaccinated 29 June 1833 (S56). The entry also names Jens Nielsen of Skee as his pleiefader (foster father) (S56).

What we do not know yet

  • At the census of 1 February 1850 Hans, then about 17, was no longer in the Skee foster household (S58). Where he was living that year has not been found.
  • The years between 1850 and 1862 are blank in our records. The next time we see him, he is a young man working at Bjernedegaard in Bjernede parish (Chapter 2).
