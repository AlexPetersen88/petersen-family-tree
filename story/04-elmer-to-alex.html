Chapter 4 — From Elmer to Alex (1906–present)

This chapter carries the line from the Minnesota farm to today. It uses Minnesota death indexes, Find a Grave (secondary), obituaries, and family testimony. Living members of the current household are left out on purpose.

Elmer Hamilton Petersen and Caroline Schultz

Elmer Hamilton Petersen was born on 24 March 1906 in Redwood County. He was the son of Hans Peter Petersen and Maren Sophia Jensen (S14, S15). The Minnesota death index lists his mother's maiden name as Jensen (S14). He appears as Elmer H., age 4, in 1910 and age 13 in 1920, on his father's Brookville Township farm (S28, S29).

His Minnesota birth index entry records him as "Babe" Petersen, mother Jensen, on the same date (S94); later records call him Elmer (S14, S28).

His wife was Caroline Alvina Minnie Schultz, born on 21 March 1908 in Sundown Township, another township of Redwood County (S16). Her death record lists her mother's maiden name as Geske (S17). Their son Donald's record lists Donald's mother's maiden name as Schultz (S19). Find a Grave gives 1931 as the year Elmer and Caroline married (S15, S16). No marriage record has been found yet.

They had two sons, Darvin and Donald (S15, S18, S19).

Darvin Wayne Petersen was born on 28 March 1932 in rural Evan, Minnesota (S278). Evan is a small village in neighboring Brown County (, 44.3540, -94.8410); the exact farm where he was born has not been identified (places/evan-mn-rural.md).Map · EvanGoogleThe village. The farm where Darvin was born is not known. He served in the US Air Force during the Korean War and went on to a career in aviation (S278). A veterans' burial listing gives his rank as first lieutenant; his obituary does not mention it, so that detail is less certain (S279). On 27 June 1954 he married Anna Maye Hansen at Bethany Lutheran Church in Morgan (S278, S280). Anna Maye was born on 3 October 1933 in rural Morgan (S280). They raised five children: Dwight, Daniel, Diana, Douglas, and Deborah (S281). Darvin died on 29 June 2007 in Wolfeboro, New Hampshire (, 43.5848, -71.2122; where in town is not known; places/wolfeboro-nh.md), survived by his wife and all five children; his only brother, Donald, had died before him (S278).Map · WolfeboroGoogleThe town only. No hospital or house. He was brought home and buried in Bethany Lutheran Church Cemetery in Morgan, where his parents rest (S278, S279, S287). The cemetery is the churchyard on the same grounds as the church pinned above; that it shares the church campus is well supported but not yet checked against a cemetery GPS point, and the row of his grave has not been pinned (places/bethany-lutheran-morgan.md). Anna Maye died on 10 May 2014 in Pownal, Cumberland County, Maine (, 43.8923, -70.1835; town only, where in town is not known) (S280, S288; places/pownal-me.md), and was buried in the same Bethany Lutheran Church Cemetery (S288); her grave row is likewise not pinned (places/bethany-lutheran-morgan.md).Map · PownalGoogleThe town only. Elmer died on 24 October 1964 in Redwood County (S14, S15). Caroline died on 8 August 1984 (S16, S17). They share plot North 9 in Bethany Lutheran Church Cemetery in Morgan (S15, S16), about 13 miles straight-line from Redwood Falls.

Donald Alvin Petersen and Janice Elaine Dohrmann

Donald Alvin Petersen was born on 23 July 1935 in Redwood County (S18, S19). He served in the US Army from 15 March 1955 to 14 March 1957, with the 82nd Airborne (S1, S2). His gravestone reads "SP 3 US ARMY" (S18).

Janice Elaine Dohrmann was born on 23 July 1938 in Sleepy Eye, Minnesota (, 44.2972, -94.7242; town only — the birth house has not been pinned) (S1, S2, S148, S149, S301; places/sleepy-eye-mn.md), to Wesley Edward Dohrmann and Ethel Bertha (Neubauer) Dohrmann (S1, S2, S148, S149).Map · Sleepy EyeGoogleThe town. Diederich, born in New Ulm, Minnesota (, 44.3140, -94.4614; town only — the birth house has not been pinned), on 17 June 1869; they married on 7 November 1889 in Sleepy Eye (Map · New UlmGoogleThe town. Henry’s own parents were Heinrich W. Dohrmann and Caroline, whose maiden name is still open (S329–S335). When Henry came to America is an open conflict, and both years stand on record: his 1946 obituary, a Weser passenger list, and the 1875 census point to 1873 (S342, S343, S344), while the 1920 census gives 1878 (S345). Neither year is settled. His obituary says the family lived about two years in Chicago, Illinois (, 41.8756, -87.6244; city only — no address is known) before moving on to Brown County and Leavenworth (S344).Map · ChicagoGoogleThe city only. Ethel was born 27 November 1911 in Brown County and died 7 June 1994 in Brown County; her mother’s maiden name was Gent (S148, S149). She and Wesley, who married in 1935, rest in Home Cemetery in Sleepy Eye (S89, S150). A Find a Grave bio for Wesley that says he married “Ethel Gent” misstates Ethel’s maiden name (S89). Both readings are on record: the Find a Grave bio says Gent; the Minnesota birth and death indexes say Neubauer, with Gent as her mother's maiden name (S148, S149). The indexes settle it. She and Donald married on 14 September 1957 in Brown County, Minnesota (S300). Her obituaries place the ceremony at the Methodist church in Sleepy Eye — today’s Faith United Methodist Church at 321 1st Ave S (, 44.2944, -94.7225); the building is well identified, but that the 1957 service used this same lot is not proven from a register (S1, S2, S301; places/sleepy-eye-methodist.md).Map · Faith UMCGoogleToday's church building. That the 1957 service used this lot is not proven. Her obituary says they farmed in Paxton Township as the second generation on the family farm (S1). Paxton Township is a township of Redwood County. For scale, Sleepy Eye, where they married, lies about 26 miles straight-line from Redwood Falls and about 13 miles from Morgan.

Their first son, Randy Don, was born on 30 July 1958 and died on 18 October 1958 in Redwood County, not yet three months old (S18, S283, S290). He was buried in Redwood Falls Cemetery, where his stone reads "2 1/2 Months" (S289). The cemetery lies off North Swain Street on the north side of Redwood Falls (, 44.5510, -95.1000); where in the cemetery his small stone stands has not been pinned (places/redwood-falls-cemetery.md).Map · Redwood Falls CemeteryGoogleThe cemetery. Randy's stone and Janice's grave are not pinned. Four more sons followed: Rick, Michael Don Petersen, Chad, and Bill (S282). Michael was born on 5 August 1961 (S0, S2). Donald and Janice had five sons in all (S282).

Donald died on 13 June 1980 at age 44 and is buried in Redwood Falls Cemetery (S18, S19). On 28 June 1986 Janice married Loren Jacobsen (S301). She died on 18 April 2025 at Vista Prairie at Garnette Gardens, 511 South DeKalb Street in Redwood Falls (, 44.5356, -95.0981) — an active care facility, pinned as her death place only (S301; places/garnette-gardens-redwood-falls.md).Map · Garnette GardensGoogleDeath place only. Not a residence, and not a grave. She was buried in Redwood Falls Cemetery, the same grounds pinned above for Randy and Donald; cemetery-level burial is well supported, but no Find a Grave memorial for her has been found, so no individual grave pin is given (S301, S302; places/redwood-falls-cemetery.md). Her obituary lists her four living sons as of 2025: Rick (Kim) and Mike (Mary) of Redwood Falls, Chad (Heather) of River Falls, Wisconsin, and Bill (Kimberlee) of Hutchinson, Minnesota (S282).

Michael and Alex

Michael Don Petersen is Alex Petersen's father (S0). Michael confirmed the family link back to Hans Peter Petersen and Maren Sophia Jensen (S0). Alex then matched the Danish records to "our Hans" (S30).

That is where this first telling of the story ends: with Alex, five generations after a twenty-year-old from Bjernede gave his name to the Copenhagen police and set out for America (S83).
