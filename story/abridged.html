The Petersen Family Story — Short Version

An easy-reading telling of the full chapters. Here, findings cleared by the Genealogist are told as plain fact. Nothing has been added that the records do not say.

1. A Boy Called Hans Pedersen (1833–1847)

Hans Pedersen was born on 25 February 1833 at Valsømagle, a village in Haraldsted parish on the Danish island of Zealand. He was baptized on Palm Sunday, 31 March, in Haraldsted church, about two miles from the village.

His mother was an unmarried servant girl from Valsømagle. Her name was Ane Marie Hansdatter. (One early baptism line was once read as Clausdatter; that reading has been set aside.) Who her parents were is still unknown. The baptism names Peder Johansen of Ringsted as the father the mother named, and the confirmation fourteen years later repeats it. No later record proves it. Ringsted lay about four miles away. In the 1834 census of Ringsted town, a servant named Ane Marie Hansdatter, 27, and a servant named Peter Johansen, 30, both unmarried, show up on the same street, Sankt Hans Gade. They may be the same two people, but that is not proven: they were not listed in the same household, and no marriage has been found.

Hans did not grow up with his mother. By his first birthday he was a foster child in the village of Skee, three miles from where he was born. He lived in the cottage of Jens Nielsen, a cottager and wheelwright, and his wife Kirsten Johansdatter. The censuses of 1834, 1840, and 1845 all find him there, first as a foster child and then as a foster son. Years later Hans was a wheelwright himself. The records do not say who taught him, but he grew up in a wheelwright's home.

On 11 April 1847 he was confirmed in Haraldsted church as "Hans Pedersen of Skee." The entry sums up his childhood in one line: his foster father Jens Nielsen of Skee, his mother Ane Marie Hansdatter, and the named father Peder Johansen.

By 1850 he had left the Skee household. The next twelve years are blank in the records.

2. Bjernede: Marriage, Children, and Loss (1862–1884)

On 14 October 1862 Hans, now a wheelwright, married Ane Rasmusdatter in Bjernede church, about eight miles from where he was born. She was born on 17 June 1843. Her parents have not been found.

Their first son was born on 17 January 1863 and died the next day, before he was named. Their second son was born on 28 February 1864 and baptized in June as Hans Peder Pedersen. He is the Hans Peter who went to Minnesota. A third son, Rasmus, was born in December 1866 and died on 5 February 1867. The dates make him about eight weeks old, but the burial book seems to say twelve days.

Seven months later, on 11 September 1867, the father died at 34 and was buried in Bjernede churchyard. Hans Peter was three and a half.

In 1870 the census shows Ane as a 27-year-old widow living in Bjernede with her son "Hans Peter Pedersen, 6." That spring she married Hans Rasmussen, a much older widower. They had two children, Rasmus Peter (1871) and Ane Marie (1874). Hans Peter was confirmed on Palm Sunday 1878, and in 1880 he was still living at home as a stepson, age 15.

In the summer of 1883 the household came to an end. Hans Rasmussen's wife, almost surely Ane, died on 29 June, and he died a few days later. In November 1884 their property in Bjernede was sold to a miller from Gyrstinge for 13,000 kroner. Part of the money was kept for the two young half-siblings, who went to foster homes. Hans Peter was not named in the sale. By then he was already gone.

3. Hans Peter Crosses to Minnesota (1884–1922)

On 16 April 1884 the Copenhagen police wrote down "Hans Peter Petersen" of Bjernede, age 20, bound for Springfield, Minnesota, traveling alone. Copenhagen is about 40 miles from Bjernede. The record does not say why he left or what ship he took.

He settled in Brookville Township, Redwood County, about 22 miles from Springfield. Around 1891 he married Maren Sophia Jensen, born in November 1872, daughter of Nels Peter Jensen and Anne Marie Hansen Jensen. Two censuses say she was born in Minnesota and one says Denmark.

By 1900 Hans was a farmer with five children. By 1910 he owned his farm, and by 1920 he ran it as a dairy farm. Together they had eight children: Nels, Rudolph (listed as Hans O. as a baby), Annie, Louis, Mary, Ethel Mathilda, Elmer, and Olga. Ethel married Werner Jensen in 1923 and died in 1929. Olga, the youngest, was born in 1911. Elmer, born on 24 March 1906 and first entered in the birth index as "Babe," carries our line.

In September 1922 the local paper reported that Hans P. Petersen, 55, of Brookville Township, about two miles east of the Brookville church, had died suddenly of heart trouble in the afternoon while sitting in a chair. He had been ill for some time. He is buried at Dannebod Cemetery, the burial ground of the township's Danish church. Maren Sophia is buried there too, and her death is given as 1937.

4. From Elmer to Alex (1906–today)

Elmer Hamilton Petersen grew up on his father's Brookville farm. He married Caroline Alvina Minnie Schultz, born in 1908 in Sundown Township, in about 1931. They had two sons, Darvin (1932–2007) and Donald. Elmer died in 1964 and Caroline in 1984. They share a plot in Bethany Lutheran Church Cemetery in Morgan.

Donald Alvin Petersen was born on 23 July 1935 and served in the Army's 82nd Airborne from 1955 to 1957. On 14 September 1957 he married Janice Elaine Dohrmann at the Methodist church in Sleepy Eye. She was the daughter of Wesley Dohrmann and Ethel Bertha Neubauer. They farmed in Paxton Township as the second generation on the family farm. Their first son, Randy, was born and died in 1958. Michael Don Petersen was born on 5 August 1961. Donald died in 1980 at 44. Janice later married Loren Jacobsen and died in Redwood Falls on 18 April 2025, at 86.

Michael is Alex's father. Michael confirmed the link back to Hans Peter and Maren Sophia, and Alex matched it to the Danish church books. That brings the story to Alex, five generations after a twenty-year-old from Bjernede gave his name to the Copenhagen police and set out for America.
