# Chapter 4 — From Elmer to Alex (1906–present)

*This chapter carries the line from the Minnesota farm to today. It uses Minnesota death indexes, Find a Grave (secondary), obituaries, and family testimony. Living members of the current household are left out on purpose.*

## Elmer Hamilton Petersen and Caroline Schultz

Elmer Hamilton Petersen was born on 24 March 1906 in Redwood County. He was the son of Hans Peter Petersen and Maren Sophia Jensen (S14, S15). The Minnesota death index lists his mother's maiden name as Jensen (S14). He appears as Elmer H., age 4, in 1910 and age 13 in 1920, on his father's Brookville Township farm (S28, S29).

His wife was Caroline Alvina Minnie Schultz, born on 21 March 1908 in Sundown Township, Redwood County (S16). Her death record lists her mother's maiden name as Geske (S17). Their son Donald's record lists Donald's mother's maiden name as Schultz (S19). Find a Grave gives 1931 as the year Elmer and Caroline married (S15, S16). No marriage record has been found yet.

They had two sons. Darvin Wayne Petersen lived from 1932 to 2007 (S15, S18). Donald Alvin was born in 1935 (S18, S19).

Elmer died on 24 October 1964 in Redwood County (S14, S15). Caroline died on 8 August 1984 (S16, S17). They share plot North 9 in Bethany Lutheran Church Cemetery in Morgan (S15, S16).

## Donald Alvin Petersen and Janice Elaine Dohrmann

Donald Alvin Petersen was born on 23 July 1935 in Redwood County (S18, S19). He served in the US Army from 15 March 1955 to 14 March 1957, with the 82nd Airborne (S1, S2). His gravestone reads "SP 3 US ARMY" (S18).

Janice Elaine Dohrmann was born on 23 July 1938 in Sleepy Eye, Minnesota, to Wesley and Ethel (Gent) Dohrmann (S1, S2). She and Donald married on 14 September 1957 at the Methodist Church in Sleepy Eye (S1, S2). Her obituary says they farmed in Paxton Township as the second generation on the family farm (S1).

Their first son, Randy Don, was born and died in 1958 (S18). Their son **Michael Don Petersen** was born on 5 August 1961 (S0, S2).

Donald died on 13 June 1980 at age 44 and is buried in Redwood Falls Cemetery (S18, S19). In 1986 Janice married Loren Jacobsen (S1). She died on 18 April 2025 in Redwood Falls, at age 86 (S1, S2, S3, S4).

## Michael and Alex

Michael Don Petersen is Alex Petersen's father (S0). Michael confirmed the family link back to Hans Peter Petersen and Maren Sophia Jensen (S0). Alex then matched the Danish records to "our Hans" (S30).

That is where this first telling of the story ends: with Alex, five generations after a twenty-year-old from Bjernede gave his name to the Copenhagen police and set out for America (S34).
