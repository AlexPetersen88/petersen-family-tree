# Chapter 3 — Hans Peter Crosses to Minnesota (1884–1922)

*This chapter covers Hans Peter Petersen (P6) and Maren Sophia Jensen (P7). It draws on Danish emigration and church records, US censuses, Minnesota death records, and Find a Grave. Find a Grave is a secondary source. Michael Don Petersen has confirmed that Hans Peter and Maren Sophia were Elmer's parents.*

## Leaving through Copenhagen, April 1884

On 16 April 1884, the Copenhagen Police emigration register recorded "Hans Peter Petersen" of Bjernede, Sorø, age 20, occupation read as servant (medium confidence), heading for Springfield, Minn., under contract no. 1015 Y, with no companions listed on that contract (S83; earlier index summary S34). The register does not say why he left, and this story does not guess. Every later American census agrees that he came in 1884 (S27, S28).

He was about two months past his twentieth birthday (S31, S34). His father had died when he was three (S37). At the time he left, his mother and stepfather were already gone (S45, S46), but the 1884 sale of his stepfather's property was still months away (S50).

The records do not show the route he took from his US arrival to Redwood County, Minnesota.

## Marriage to Maren Sophia Jensen, about 1891

Hans Peter's wife was Maren Sophia Jensen. She was born in November 1872 in Minnesota (S27, S13). Her parents were Nels Peter Jensen and Anne Marie Hansen Jensen (S13). The 1910 census says the couple had been married 19 years, which puts the wedding in about 1891 (S28). No marriage record has been found yet.

## The farm in Brookville Township

**1900.** On 19 June 1900, the census taker for Brookville Township, Redwood County, listed the "Hans Peterson" household (S27). Hans was born in February 1864 in Denmark. He had come in 1884, had been in the US 16 years, and had taken the first step toward citizenship (marked "Na"). His occupation was farmer (S27). His wife was listed as "Mary," and the children were Nels A. (born October 1891), Hans O. (May 1893), Annie H. (January 1895), Louis C. (October 1896), and Mary S. (May 1898) (S27).

**1910.** On 23 April 1910, the family was still in Brookville Township. Hans P., 45, was a farmer who **owned his farm** (S28). Maren S., 37, born in Minnesota, was recorded as the mother of seven children, all seven living (S28). The children at home were Rudolph H. 16, Anna H. 15, Louis C. 13, Marie E. 11, Ethel M. 8, and Elmer H. 4 (S28).

**1920.** In January 1920, Hans P., 55, born in Denmark with Danish as his mother tongue, still **owned his farm** in Brookville Township and was now described as a **dairy farmer** (S29). At home were Maren S., 47, and Anna H. 25, Ethel M. 17, Elmer H. 13, and Olga M. 8 (S29). This census gives Maren's birthplace as Denmark, but the 1900 and 1910 censuses both say Minnesota (S27, S28, S29).

## The children

The children named in the records are:

- **Nels A.**, born October 1891 (S27)
- **Annie (Anna) H.**, born January 1895 (S27, S28, S29)
- **Louis C.**, born October 1896 (S27, S28)
- **Mary S.**, born May 1898 (S27). The 1910 census lists a daughter "Marie E.," 11 (S28).
- **Rudolph H. Petersen**, born 8 May 1893 in Minnesota; mother Jensen; died 13 April 1964 in Hennepin County (S95). He appears as "Rudolph H.," 16, in 1910 (S28). The 1900 census lists a son "Hans O.," born May 1893 (S27); Genealogist treats that as matching Rudolph’s MNHS dates (S95).
- **Ethel Mathilda Petersen**, born 15 January 1902 in Brookville Township (S92, S152). She appears as Ethel M., 8, in 1910 (S28). She married Werner Jensen on 3 January 1923 (S152). She died on 20 October 1929 at the Redwood Falls hospital after an operation; the funeral was held at the Hans Jensen home in Brookville, then Brookville Lutheran Church, with burial in the church cemetery; Rev. H. Madsen of Sleepy Eye officiated (S152). Survivors named in the notice and cleared for this story include her mother (“Mrs. Hans Petersen” of Brookville), her sister Olga, and her brother Elmer (S152). Other sisters’ married names in that notice are unverified and are not used here.
- **Elmer Hamilton Petersen**, born 24 March 1906 in Redwood County. The Minnesota birth index registers him as "Babe" Petersen, mother Jensen (S94); later records use Elmer (S14, S15, S28). He is our line (Chapter 4).
- **Olga Margaret Petersen**, born 28 February 1911 in Redwood County; mother Jensen (S93). She appears as Olga M., 8, in 1920 (S29). Death year 1935 is still from Find a Grave only (S15).

Altogether, seven children were living in 1910 (S28), and Olga was born afterward (S93, S29), for eight children.

## Hans Peter's death

In September 1922 the *Redwood Gazette* reprinted a Morgan Messenger notice: Hans P. Petersen, age 55, of Brookville Township about two miles east of the Brookville church, died suddenly about 4:30 in the afternoon of heart trouble and complications while sitting in a chair. He had been ailing for some time; funeral arrangements were not yet made. He was survived by his wife and several children, unnamed in the notice (S145).

**Open conflict:** the newspaper prints **Hans P.**; a Minnesota Historical Society death index lists **Hans T.** Petersen with date 1922-09-06 (candidate only). Genealogist has not resolved the middle initial, and this story does not treat 1922-09-06 as a proven death day (S145).

He is buried at Dannebod Cemetery in Brookville Township, Redwood County (S12). The cemetery dates from 1876 and was the burial ground of the Danish Fredsminde church, built in 1889 (S20). The wording on his stone has not yet been transcribed.

Find a Grave places Maren Sophia’s death in 1937 and her burial at Dannebod (S13). A Minnesota Historical Society death confirmation for that year was not approved in Genealogist’s MN Pack #5, so the 1937 date stays secondary for now.
