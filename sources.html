Hans Peter Petersen’s origin is family-confirmed (Alex, 2026-11-03, S30): born 28 Feb 1864 in Bjernede, Sorø amt, Denmark. His Danish parents, stepfather, and siblings stay record-sourced only. Ane’s 29 Jun 1883 death is entered as “Ane Marie Rasmussen,” wife for the second time of Hans Rasmussen, and is matched to Ane Rasmusdatter by inference (S45). Hans Rasmussen died about 2 Jul 1883, age 83; the birth year leans 1801 and he is not linked to a ~1833 candidate (S46). Rasmus Peter’s 1885 confirmation is record-sourced (S47); an 18 Jun 1889 emigration to Sleepy Eye, contract 4232 Y, is candidate / unverified (S49). Ane Marie’s 1888 confirmation names a foster father (S48); her fate after 1888 is unknown, and the baptism day is 10 Jan 1875 in one record and 11 Jan 1875 in the other.
