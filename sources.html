Hans Peter Petersen’s origin is family-confirmed (Alex, 2026-11-03, S30): born 28 Feb 1864 in Bjernede, Sorø amt, Denmark, baptized 5 Jun 1864. His Danish parents, stepfather, and siblings are record-sourced only and are not family-confirmed. That includes an unnamed son born 17 Jan 1863 and died 18 Jan 1863 without baptism (S42), and Rasmus Pedersen born 10 Dec 1866, baptized 23 Jan 1867, died 5 Feb 1867 (S43, S41). The 1870 census (S44) shows widow Ane, 27, and Hans Peter, 6, alone. A namesake servant about 19 on another page of that census is not this Hans Peter. An unreliable “4th Barn” remark is not used to add another sibling. Hans Rasmussen’s birth year 1801 vs 1831 is unresolved.
