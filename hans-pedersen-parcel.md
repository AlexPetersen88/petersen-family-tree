# Hans Pedersen (hjulmand/husmand, 1833–1867): his parcel. Land-record pass, 2026-11-03

**Bottom line: NO land record naming OUR Hans Pedersen as owner was found.** The Bjernede realregister (1850+) lists every tinglyst owner of Bjernedehuse matr. 1–6. Hans Pedersen is not among them. The most likely explanation (UNVERIFIED) is that he was a *lejehusmand*: he rented a house or lived on someone else's plot, so no deed was ever registered in his name. people.json is UNCHANGED.

## What Bjernedehuse actually was (Verified by reading the images)
Bjernedehuse fol 263–270 (imgs 40788093–40788100) holds about 6 **Selvejerbolsteder**. Each had old hartkorn of 8 tdr 5 skp (the original bolstad), reduced to about 1 td hartkorn per plot. Sorø Akademi sold all of them in one batch: deeds dated 7 Nov 1855, tinglyst 22 Nov 1855, Pantebog T.
- 1a/1b (img 093): Christen Bertelsen, later Jens Pedersen…, J.P. Andersen and others
- 2 (img 094): Hans Larsen, then Lars Larsen in 1858
- 3 (img 095): Søren Olsen; 3b split 1864/65 (Thomas Larsen)
- 3b "Selvejerhuus" (img 096): Søren Olsen → Thomas Larsen 1858 → Hans Christensen 1865 → Niels Hansen 1865 → Jens Pedersen 1867(?) → Jacob Olsen 1872…
- 4 (img 097): Peder Pedersen; widow Marie Pedersdatter 1871
- **5a (img 098, fol 268): Skjøde fra Sorø Akademi til Hans Rasmussen, 7/22 Nov 1855 (T fol 97).** The next entry is a skifte-related transfer to "Müller J.T. Mathiesen, Gyrstinge", dated 12/1 1884 and tinglyst 13/1 1884. Hand read by eye.
- 6 (img 100): Peil (Poul?) Sørensen 1855, then his widow, then Niels Jørgensen in 1874.
These are small farms, not the houses of craftsmen husmænd. Hans Pedersen does not appear as an owner on any of them.

## Candidate (UNVERIFIED): stepfather Hans Rasmussen and Bjernedehuse matr. 5a
- Our records show Hans Rasmussen, Ane's 2nd husband, died Jul 1883 aged 83 "i Bjerne Huse".
- The owner of matr. 5a from 1855 is a Hans Rasmussen, and the next transfer (1884) follows his death. The timing fits.
- If this is the same man, widow Ane and Hans Peter (b. 1864) probably lived on matr. 5a Bjernedehuse after Ane remarried (after 1867), not on Hans Pedersen's own place.
- NOT proven: Hans Rasmussen is a common name. Needed: the 1884 skifte or skøde text (Skøde- og panteprotokol vol. VV?, fol 3x), or a census matching the household to 5a.

### Verdict for 5a
- **Verified:** the owner of matr. 5a Bjernedehuse at death (1883) was bolsmand Hans Rasmussen, wife Ane Marie Rasmussen (predeceased), minor heirs Rasmus Peter (P21, b. 1871) and Ane Marie (P22, b. 1874). This is exactly Ane Rasmusdatter's second family (P20/P19), matching the 1883 burials and 1885/1888 confirmations. The realregister shows no owner between the 1855 Sorø Akademi deed "til Hans Rasmussen" and the 1884 estate sale, and the 1884 deed says the property had belonged to the deceased Hans Rasmussen, so the 1855 buyer = the stepfather (strong; the 1855 deed text itself, vol. T, is NOT digitized — Daisy "Start bestilling").
- **Consequence:** after Ane remarried (1870), she and Hans Peter (b. 1864) lived on matr. 5a, a ~1 td hartkorn selvejer-bolsted. It was never Hans Pedersen's property; Hans Pedersen (d. 1867) was not the owner.
- **Rejected:** 5a as Hans Pedersen's own plot; the Jan-1884 date (it is Nov 1884); indsidder Hans Andersen (1867 lease on 5a) is a different man (Andersen, alive post-1867) — but note a sub-tenant lived on 5a from Dec 1867.
- Age conflict for Hans Rasmussen (83 at death vs 1831 on 1870 remarriage) still UNRESOLVED; deed gives no age.
