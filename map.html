Denmark On the map

Bjernede Kirke, Bjernede Kirkevej 1A, 4180 Sorø. Zealand’s only round church.

On 14 October 1862 Hans Pedersen and Ane Rasmusdatter were married here (S36). Hans Peter was baptized here on 5 June 1864 (S31) and confirmed on 14 April 1878. Hans Pedersen was buried in this churchyard on 17 September 1867 (S37), and the infant Rasmus on 10 February 1867 (S41). No grave from those 1867 burials is known to survive. The place to stand is the churchyard, not a marked stone.

Place and these events: high. Grave location: unverified. Pin 55.4621, 11.6251.

Haraldsted Præstegård, Haraldstedvej 91, Haraldsted, 4100 Ringsted.

On 21 May 1797 a betrothal (trolovelse) was recorded here: the widower Hans Pedersen and the girl Maren Hansdatter of Valsømagle (S222). The event and the place are high. Whether this couple were Ane Marie Hansdatter’s parents stays a candidate. Her baptism has not been found, so they are not locked as her parents.

The pin is the address point, about 80 metres southeast of the church, not a building outline. GPS for that point: medium. It is a working private residence and parish office. Stand on Haraldstedvej or on the churchyard side. The grounds are private.

Pin 55.4910, 11.7692.

Sct. Hansgade (Sankt Hans Gade), 4100 Ringsted. The pin is the middle of the street. The exact 1834 door is unverified.

The 1834 census places Peter Johansen, about 30, unmarried, a tjenestekarl, on Sankt Hans Gade (S64). Genealogist Decision B clears him as the same man as Peder Johansen of Ringsted, the named father of Hans. The pin is that street, not a door.

An Ane Marie Hansdatter in the same census is a candidate match only (S63), in a separate servants’ entry. No shared household is claimed.

Street: high. Peter Johansen’s 1834 residence, at street level: high. Exact door: unverified. Pin 55.4445, 11.7908.

Hamlet overlook, not the house. Public road near Stokholtsvej, about 1.2 km east-southeast of Bjernede Kirke.

The records use the names Bjernedegaardshuse and Bjernedehuse for the family’s years in this hamlet. An 1884 deed names matr. no. 5a of Bjernedehuse (S50). This pin is an overlook from the road. It is not the house, and it is not the 5a plot. The deed shows matr. 5a belonged to Hans Rasmussen until his death in 1883. Hans Pedersen did not own it. Whether Hans Peter lived on 5a is not proven.

Place-name: high. GPS of this overlook: low to medium. No house is pinned. Pin 55.4597, 11.6419.

Minnesota On the map

Dannebod Cemetery, 180th Street, Brookville Township, near Cobden, Redwood County, Minnesota.

Hans Peter Petersen (1864–1922) is buried here (S12). The cemetery location is high. The burial is secondary, from Find a Grave, so it stays medium confidence. The marker is on the north side of 180th Street, with the Fredsminde church monument behind it. That church stood from 1889 to 1961.

Find a Grave also places Maren Sophia’s 1937 burial here. That date and burial stay secondary (S13).

The Brookville Township farm is not on this map. Its site is unverified. Pin 44.31115, -94.89040.
