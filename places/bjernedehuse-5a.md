- **Record name:** Matr. no. 5a of Bjernedehuse, Bjernede Sogn. The place is tree-true. Hans living on matr. 5a stays unproven.
- **Modern:** The strip south of Stokholtsvej is still matr. 5a. Stand near Stokholtsvej 35, the property that includes parcel 4. Those buildings are not the 1800s house. The pin is the parcel, not the modern house.
- **Lat/lon:** 55.4656, 11.6444. Confidence HIGH.
